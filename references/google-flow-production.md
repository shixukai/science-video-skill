# Google Flow cloud production

## Select the route and budget bilingual chains at G1

Set `design.production_route=google_flow` when the user commissions a Google Flow production. Use [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) to generate delivered video imagery and generate all delivered audio in Flow, an authorized companion cloud speech/audio service, or (only when cloud TTS is blocked or lacks conversational quality) the episode's confirmed series `Qwen3-TTS` baseline (`voice-baseline.json`) backed by `cloud_tts_blocker_evidence`. Perform editing, mixing, captioning, export and project review in Flow wherever active project tools satisfy quality and measurement requirements, using local post-production or QA tooling only for documented unmet steps. Local 3D rendering, screen capture and stock footage or music cannot supply delivered source assets on this route.

Plan complete explanatory chains before writing prompts:

- **Plan the full explanation first.** Trace the [causal chain](topic-contract.md#complete-chains-and-duration-for-engineering-and-manufacturing-topics) from the initial conditions and trigger through each necessary physical change to the result and its evidence. Include a comparison, failure case or operating limit when it helps explain why the result occurs. In the existing shot table, map each key link to what viewers see, what the narration explains and the time they need to observe it. For an invisible mechanism, show a scientifically valid observable effect or a clearly identified model, and maintain one persistent subject and spatial orientation across `2–4` consecutive shots (bridged via Flow `Frames`/`Extend` or a shared scene preamble) for multi-step physical or physiological deformations instead of swapping to an unrelated prop every `~5 s`.
- **Let the mechanism set scope and length.** Set the number of shots and total runtime from the complete explanation, natural narration and observation time in both languages. Do not impose a shot-count or runtime target or cut a causal step to fit a template, clip length, budget, or feed target. Extend a shot or add adjoining shots when a viewer needs to see the cause, change and result separately. Tighten repeated material and unrelated detours while preserving explanatory beats. If a hard platform limit or available credits cannot accommodate the complete version, report the constraint and keep the full explanation intact.
- **Bilingual pairing at G1.** Every complete Google Flow film delivers two individually playable sibling records under the same project: `episode.zh.json` (Chinese narration and captions) and `episode.en.json` (English narration and captions), sharing the same scientific picture sequence, shot order, truth register, `time_scale` annotations, and common foley/ambience/music stems.
- **Native 24 fps clip budgeting before generation.** Flow's native output stream runs at Constant Frame Rate `24/1 fps` (`~7.917 s` / `190` frames in `720p Original`; `~7.833 s` / `188` frames in `1080p Upscaled`). After trimming `0.15–0.40 s` of ramp/conditioning head and holding a `0.20–0.40 s` tail margin before drift, each raw clip yields roughly `6.8–7.4 s` of usable core. Budget every shot against `max(span_zh, span_en)` at G1: if either language exceeds `~7.0 s` on a shot (or needs longer observation), split that beat into adjacent sub-shots (`SHxxA`/`SHxxB`), generate a Flow `Extend` continuation, or bridge with tail-to-head `Frames` conditioning before final rendering. Never stretch raw clips with `setpts` slow motion, freeze tails with `tpad`, loop clips, or apply `atempo` warping to fill narration overrun.
- **Lock `design.output.fps=24` for pure Flow timelines.** Keep timeline composition and final encoding at native `24/1 fps` CFR (`1920×1080` default landscape or `1080×1920` when vertical is explicitly commissioned; honor higher explicit targets) so 24 fps Flow clips are never judder-converted to `30 fps` by frame duplication. Use non-24 rates only when an explicit higher-rate deliverable is commissioned and backed by documented frame-rate handling.
- **Mechanism and scope boundaries.** At G1, verify whether generated shots can preserve required geometry, scale, state order and continuity without turning invisible fields into photoreal objects. Apply [full-frame scope rules](realistic-production.md#declaration-structure-and-validation-scope) if changing the visual medium, report route blockers cleanly without silently switching routes, and follow [science and provenance](science-and-rights.md).

## Start with active project controls, pacing and 1080p Upscaled staging

Before generating shots, open or create the authorized Flow project in desktop web view and verify current account/region access, active Veo model tier, input modes (`Text`, `Frames`, `Ingredients`), aspect ratio, duration option, `1080p Upscaled` availability, generated audio toggle and quoted credit cost. Leave room for the `>= 0.15 s` opening hook trim. [Get started with Flow](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353333?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop), [Create videos](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353334?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop), [Models and supported features](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16352836?hl=en), [Regions](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353544?hl=en), [Credits](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234?hl=en).

- **Disk-backed prompt ledger and compaction recovery.** Before submitting any browser batch, save a versioned local ledger mapping every `shot_id` to its exact verbatim prompt, input mode, conditioning source (`Frames`/`Ingredients`), expected usable span, Flow project card/candidate index, and downloaded filename. Always re-read that disk ledger after any context compaction or session resume so shot prompts and card indices never drift.
- **Strict serial submission and anti-bot restraint.** Submit and upscale **one prompt/card serially** at calm, human-paced intervals, awaiting generation and upscale completion cleanly before starting the next request. Never burst concurrent browser tabs or rapid-fire loops. If Flow displays an anti-bot challenge (`"We noticed some unusual activity"`), CAPTCHA, sign-in barrier, regional block, or credit exhaustion, stop immediately, record the exact blocker and checkpointed card index, and ask the user; never attempt to wait out, click through, script around, or bypass any gate.
- **Reuse clean in-project assets.** Prefer existing clean project cards, saved `Frames` and verified `Ingredients` over duplicate re-uploads or blind re-rolls. Submit only the minimum authorized visual input needed, avoiding unnecessary confidential or identifiable media. [Minimal media transfer](tool-routing.md#review-media-without-repeated-uploads), [Flow data controls](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17025472?hl=en), [Flow privacy notice](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow/privacy).
- **Mandatory `1080p Upscaled` download and staging verification.** Trigger Flow's `1080p Upscaled` export for every selected shot (`720p Original` may guide candidate selection only and never enters final sample/episode assembly when HD output is required). Verify every Playwright/browser download completes cleanly (`dl.path() !== null` and `dl.failure() === null`), save to deterministic shot filenames, and run `ffprobe` on arrival to confirm actual width/height (`>= 1920×1080` for default 16:9), `24/1 fps` stream rate, playable video + `48 kHz` audio stream integrity, and clean frame count. Generate a raw full-frame contact sheet (`0 s`, `2 s`, `4 s`, `6 s`, `7.5 s` / head-mid-tail) before post-production assembly so defects never hide until G5.

## Prompting, conditioning, clean phantoms and native foley

Write every shot from its [shot card](realistic-direction.md#write-a-shot-card-before-the-prompt) first: fix the shot's [truth register](realistic-direction.md#assign-a-truth-register-to-every-shot), declared [time scale](realistic-direction.md#declare-and-verify-time-scale), scene lighting preamble, fixed camera package, and single starting-to-resulting physical transition described in [concrete physical language](realistic-direction.md#word-prompts-physically). Select candidates [science first](realistic-direction.md#generate-and-select-in-the-right-order), then continuity, phone-screen readability, optical texture and aesthetic polish.

1. **Hook sample (`0–5 s`) and G3 precede full body expansion.** Produce `SH01` (and any seamless hook join) at `1080p Upscaled` first. Trim at least the first `0.15 s` (`>= 4` frames) of any static conditioning settle so motion begins on frame 0 under [the two-second hook rule](viral-appeal-and-packaging.md#win-the-first-two-seconds) and reaches a clear visual change by `~2.0 s`. Assemble both the primary Chinese `0–5 s` hook sample and the English `0–5 s` opening check against the identical visual beats, measure with `scripts/opening_hook.py` and pass [the hook test](viral-appeal-and-packaging.md#produce-and-test-the-hook-first) + representative risk-relationship G3 before generating remaining body shots.
2. **Frameless, text-free physical staging (`>= 90%` safe zone).** Instruct clean, unlabeled, unmarked physical props, ungraduated instrument housings, or unreadable background displays per [honest instruments](realistic-direction.md#keep-numbers-instruments-and-readable-surfaces-honest) and [marking limits](visual-system.md#in-picture-markings-require-specific-consent). Never prompt for multi-digit numeric counters, on-screen labels, split-screen panels, picture-in-picture insets, decorative borders, vignette frames or UI mockups — Flow renders pseudo-glyphs and clipped overlays that break close-ups. Compose key action inside the central/upper-middle safe area (`~90%` margin clear of platform UI chrome).
3. **Clean biological cutaways vs. non-biological benchtop phantoms.** When explaining internal animal or human organ mechanics, prioritize warm, non-graphic museum/natural-history-grade 3D anatomical cutaways (`Model` register, `observation_level="not_observable"`), anchored first inside the whole-organism silhouette and tracked continuously on the same anatomical model via Flow `Frames` or `Extend` while preserving true soft-tissue compliance and circumferential deformation. Never substitute unnamed industrial hardware (metal flanges, rollers, hanging weights, pistons, measuring cups, or cross-blade extruders) for biological organs, and never hop across disconnected mechanical rigs from shot to shot. Reserve optical benchtop phantoms (borosilicate glass, clear hydrogel, tracer dye/microbeads) strictly for non-biological physics/chemistry/engineering topics or at most one explicitly introduced everyday analogy. Inspect the `4–8 s` second half of every generated clip on full-frame contact sheets and normal-speed playback specifically for late morphs, detached geometry, disappearing tracer flows, or melted textures.
4. **Concise `Audio:` clause and selective native `48 kHz` foley retention.** When Flow generates audio, end the prompt with a short, literal `Audio:` line naming only physical contact foley or real room ambience (never voiceover, spoken dialogue or background music). Inspect every staged clip's `48 kHz` audio channel: retain crisp physical contact foley (`60–100 ms` fade/J-L crossfade aligned within 1 frame of visible contact at `1.0×` speed, zero `atempo` distortion), and strictly **mute** clips containing hallucinated speech, garbled chatter, unwanted music or electrical buzz in favor of a continuous ambience bed plus clean cloud sound stems.

## Build a cloud-generated narration master for each language

Adapt the approved Chinese narration script into natural spoken English while preserving exact causal sequence, quantities, units, conditions, uncertainty bounds and conclusion scope under [claim-meaning preservation](science-and-rights.md#preserve-claim-meaning-through-production-handoffs) and [spoken narration craft](voice-reference.md#edit-narration-and-dialogue-for-the-ear). Keep both editions locked to the shared shot table under [narration-driven pacing](realistic-direction.md#let-narration-and-the-shots-job-set-its-length): organize visuals into coherent **spatial/lighting acts** (`wildlife act -> continuous anatomical/model act -> closing outdoor act`, never ping-ponging daylight and dark studio on isolated single shots), and bridge adjacent shots smoothly with natural clause flow instead of freezing after every short sentence.

- **TTS preflight and fallback priority (anti-robotic-voice rule).** Run live preflight on Flow audio or an authorized cloud speech service first (preferring modern conversational voices such as `Chirp 3 HD` or `en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural` rather than strained novel/announcer defaults). Whenever cloud TTS endpoints fail regional/auth checks (`403` / `GCP_NOT_ENABLED`) or lack a natural conversational register—and a confirmed local `Qwen3-TTS` baseline (`voice-baseline.json`, e.g., `mandarin-calm-serena-v1` `Serena` + `Qwen3-TTS` English `Ryan`/`Serena` with conversational `instruct`) is available—fall back to that verified `local_qwen` baseline and bind the hash-verified `cloud_tts_blocker_evidence` receipt in `narration.generation_record` rather than downgrading to mechanical robot-paced voices. Never silently claim local audio came from a remote cloud server.
- **Anti-AI-tone script drafting and dual `(spoken_text, caption_text)` cues.** Write the narration script as `3–5` cohesive conversational paragraphs rather than `10+` standalone couplets separated by `\n\n`. Strip prompt-header colons (`“第一步靠…：”`, `“先打破误区：”`), symmetrical `[7–9 chars]，[7–9 chars]。` slogan templates, coined shorthand (`“硬带压平”`), and semicolon caveats tacked onto unrelated shots. Maintain dynamic **clause-level single-line subtitles** (`WrapStyle=2` ZH, `WrapStyle=0` EN) aligned to natural spoken phrases instead of stagnant full-shot 2-line captions, with zero SSML/markup leakage.
- **Unchopped continuous synthesis and natural J/L-cut pacing.** Follow [continuous narration and shared chain](voice-reference.md#one-narration-master-and-one-shared-processing-chain) independently per language. Generate each episode (or `2–3` act-level paragraphs) in one continuous call at natural conversational cadence (never warping neural speech rates beyond `|rate| <= 6%`, e.g. avoiding `-14%` drawl). Do NOT slice continuous narration at every shot boundary to splice in repeated `1.5–2.2 s` artificial noise pauses: trim only lead/tail dead air and reserve `>= 0.8 s` wordless observation holds for `2–4` major structural reveals or act closures (`>= 15%` of shots). Snap visual cut points to natural breath valleys and offset cuts slightly (`0.12–0.30 s` J-cuts / L-cuts) so sentences flow organically across cuts. Master once via `scripts/prepare_voice_master.py` (`-18 LUFS` narration stem -> `-16 LUFS` / `-1.5 dBTP` final mix).
- **Audio stems and music rights.** Keep dry voice masters, retained native/cloud foley, continuous ambience and optional [Google Flow Music](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17084348?hl=en) (within verified plan rights) on traceable, separate stems so music drops cleanly under explanatory speech and never masks physical contact transients. [Flow Music account requirements](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17083868?hl=en), [Google AI Pro music benefits and usage rights](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/14534406?hl=en).
- **Canonical audio bindings.** Record `narration.voice_type="synthetic"`, `narration.execution.status="confirmed"`, actual mode (`"service"` with HTTPS `location` and `cloud_evidence`, or verified `"local_project"` with `cloud_tts_blocker_evidence` and full Qwen weights/revision evidence), actual `voice.speaker`/`voice.language` (`"Chinese"` / `"English"`), and hashed input/output receipts.

## Edit, mix, caption, export and review in Flow

- **Continuous world ambience, `150–250 ms` foley crossfades, and boundary dissolves.** Carry a continuous per-location ambience bed across all shots in the same world (no fading ambient sound to zero and back up on every cut). When retaining Flow `48 kHz` foley, apply `150–250 ms` equal-power overlaps across same-world cuts. Between distinct worlds (for example `wildlife -> 3D biological cutaway`), apply a restrained `0.20–0.30 s` (`5–7` frames) visual dissolve and smooth ambience crossfade while extending upstream trim frames so total episode timing stays locked to continuous narration; keep crisp hard cuts inside the same scene.
- **Editing and normal-speed project review.** Assemble shots cut to each [usable core](realistic-direction.md#use-each-clips-usable-core) in desktop [Scenebuilder](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16935718?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) where supported, reviewing joins, action clarity and pacing at normal speed (`1.0×`). Keep project review strictly scoped to what was genuinely watched and heard in Flow.
- **Mixing, captions and documented local gap handoff.** Apply active Flow tools such as CaptionCast or Mondo Sónico where the current account/project verifies exact frame timing, SRT output, clean language wrapping and integrated loudness accuracy. Whenever Flow lacks per-edition SRT export, `24 fps` CFR muxing control, two-pass `-16 LUFS` loudness metering, or bilingual subtitle styling, record the specific missing capability in `design.flow_postproduction_gaps`, preserve the untouched `1080p Upscaled` Flow source clips and cloud audio masters as upstream base inputs, and use local FFmpeg/adapter post-production solely for those documented gaps under [the phase adapter entry](production-checkpoints.md#start-lock-and-actual-rendering-entry). [Flow Tools and credit rules](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/17104535?hl=en), [Google's CaptionCast and Mondo Sónico showcase](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/six-new-tools-built-by-creatives/).
- **Edition-specific caption geometry.** Render synchronized subtitles from each edition's checked `caption_text`: `.zh.srt` uses `WrapStyle=2` with legible CJK sizing (`>= 20 pt` / series style margin clear of platform UI), while `.en.srt` uses `WrapStyle=0` (smart even line-breaking without orphaned single words). Inspect densest subtitle blocks, units and negations against spoken audio on real frames in both editions.
- **Covers across languages.** Generate clean text-free base artwork (`3:4` and `4:3`) and design [independent Chinese and English typographic covers](visual-system.md#design-the-two-cover-ratios-independently). Never publish a Chinese-texted cover unchanged onto an English edition.
- **Export resolution and stream fidelity.** Export each language edition separately (`*zh*.mp4` + `.zh.srt`, `*en*.mp4` + `.en.srt`) at `1920×1080` (`24/1 fps` CFR unless commissioned otherwise). Never run blind post-re-encodes over compliant direct Flow exports; whenever local assembly runs, probe streams and inspect final output bytes directly under [HD video output](quality-acceptance.md#hd-video-output). [Flow assets and downloads](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16935308?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop).

## Audit staged clips and paired editions before delivery

Do not invent ad-hoc episode JSON schemas or ad-hoc pass scripts that omit provenance or QA structure. Bind every used cloud clip and audio stem cleanly into sibling `episode.zh.json` and `episode.en.json` records:

- Record local submission/ledger proofs in `shots[].shotbook.origin.entrypoint` and `assets[].origin.entrypoint` (classifying Flow picture assets as `ai_generated`).
- Map every shot's cloud picture asset in `shots[].asset_ids` and shared foley/ambience/music stems in `design.render_asset_ids`.
- Before delivery, run four checks across the staged clips and sibling exports:
  1. `python -X utf8 scripts/google_flow_audit.py measure <zh_export.mp4> --episode-zh <episode.zh.json> --en-video <en_export.mp4> --episode-en <episode.en.json> --report <new-flow-audit.json>` (or `--clips-only` at clip-staging time) to screen `720p` leaks, `24->30 fps` cadence judder, clip duration overruns vs native clip length, speech-rate rush, missing observation holds, and `zh`/`en` shot-order or duration divergence.
  2. `python -X utf8 scripts/opening_hook.py measure <export.mp4> --captions <captions.srt> --preview-dir <preview_dir> --report <hook-report.json>` on both editions' `0–5 s` hooks and finals.
  3. `python -X utf8 scripts/realistic_audit.py measure <export.mp4> --shots <shots.json> --delivery-fps 24 --report <realistic-audit.json>` on both `24 fps` exports.
  4. `python -X utf8 scripts/check_episode.py <episode.zh.json> --stage delivery --report <zh-delivery.json>` and `python -X utf8 scripts/check_episode.py <episode.en.json> --stage delivery --report <en-delivery.json>`.
- **Review mechanism coverage at G2 and G5.** In the existing explanation review, match the planned causal links to actual timecodes in each edition. A viewer should be able to state what causes the effect, what changes in between and how the result follows. Return skipped, visually unsupported or rushed links for added shots or observation time. Check the complete sequence at normal speed in both languages.
- **Independent bilingual G5 and SynthID provenance.** Have reviewers proficient in Chinese and English respectively watch and listen to each complete export continuously at `1.0×` speed, recording separate review identities/observations (a prompt log, file list, or `ffprobe` pass never passes G5). Preserve SynthID and provider provenance per [Flow watermarking rules](https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16353333?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop), and disclose AI-generated media accurately at authorized platform submission under [publication declaration rules](production-checkpoints.md#declare-ai-generated-content-at-publication).

### Diagnose by symptom

| Symptom | Likely root cause | Concrete fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ffprobe` shows `1280×720` on staged clips | Downloaded `720p Original` tile instead of waiting for `1080p Upscaled` | Re-export `1080p Upscaled` in Flow, check `dl.path()`/`dl.failure()` and re-run `ffprobe` (`1920×1080`) before assembly |
| Soft judder / duplicate every 4th frame | Native `24 fps` Flow clips forced onto a `30 fps` timeline | Lock `design.output.fps=24` CFR cleanly; pass `--delivery-fps 24` to `realistic_audit.py` and re-mux from source clips |
| Unnatural slow-motion, frozen clip tails, or warped `atempo` foley | Shot narration (`max(span_zh, span_en)`) exceeded usable `~7.0 s` core | Split beat into `SHxxA`/`SHxxB`, use Flow `Extend` or `Frames` bridge at G1/G2; remove `setpts`/`tpad`/`atempo` hacks |
| Rushed, breathless narration & zero pauses | `rate` sped up past `0%` or `>240` CJK chars/min (`>160` WPM) to cram template slots | Restore unwarped natural delivery (`|rate| <= 6%`), write concise conversational prose, and leave purposeful `>=0.8 s` holds on `>= 15%` of shots |
| AI-flavored narration, per-shot pitch resets, awkward `1.5–2 s` dead pauses, or jarring brick-wall cuts | Per-shot `\n\n` script splitting, heavy TTS rate drawl (`-14%`), sliced `1.8 s` artificial silence padding, symmetrical `8+8` couplets, prompt colons (`第一步：`), outdoor/studio ping-pong cuts, or foley fading to zero per cut | Merge into `3–5` natural conversational paragraphs; synthesize unchopped via confirmed calm `Qwen3-TTS` (`Serena`/`Ryan`) when cloud TTS lacks naturalness/access; group worlds into acts with `0.25 s` boundary dissolves, `200 ms` foley crossfades, continuous ambience beds, and `0.12–0.30 s` J/L-cuts |
| `zh` vs `en` durations drift far apart or miss referents | Shared shot sequence not budgeted to `max(span_zh, span_en)` | Align English spoken pacing and observation holds per shot to match shared picture beats; keep identical shot IDs |
| `"We noticed some unusual activity"` / `BOT_CHECK_HIT` | Parallel multi-tab Flow submissions or rapid polling | Stop immediately and report blocker cleanly (never bypass); resume only with strict 1-by-1 serial queuing and cooldown |
| Swapped shot prompts or card indices mid-batch | Context compaction lost prompt/card mapping in memory | Keep versioned prompt/card ledger on disk and re-read it verbatim before every single submission or download |
| Cloud TTS fails (`403` / `GCP_NOT_ENABLED`) or misreads symbols/terms | Missing TTS preflight or single uncleaned text string sent to TTS & SRT | Preflight authorized cloud speech endpoints before G3; maintain `(spoken_text, caption_text)` pairs |
| Garbled dial numbers, PiP borders, melted tissue, or rigid industrial stand-ins | Asked Flow for dense labels, inset frames, graphic gore, or mechanical dies for biological organs | Use museum-grade soft-tissue anatomical cutaways for biology (glass/hydrogel phantoms only for non-biological physics), blank unmarked framing and `4–8 s` tail checks |
| Clashing gibberish audio or dull clipped loudness | Unfiltered Veo baked-in speech/music left unmuted or wrong LUFS chain | Mute speech/music clips, crossfade clean `48 kHz` foley `60–100 ms`, mix under continuous room tone + `-16 LUFS` voice master |
