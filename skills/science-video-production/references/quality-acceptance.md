# Quality Acceptance: References, Behavior, Evidence, and Return Conditions

Check the actual project and execution records against the established [production route](tool-routing.md#lock-the-primary-tool-by-final-film-style), then review the applicable items on this page. Conclusions must point to current picture, sound, or specific evidence. Record technical passes and actual-review results separately.

<a id="先确定可追溯的参照"></a>
## Establish Traceable References First

Choose structural, appearance, and motion references actually viewed for this film's medium, recording source/version, purpose, and motion intervals. Structural material supports scientific relationships; style images support appearance; verify rights separately. Do not impose historical 2D illustration on realistic/3D work. Motion references require continuous viewing; unviewed parts remain unverified.

Compare references and exports side by side at comparable shot sizes, checking contours, proportions, connections/contacts, and function item by item. Record gaps, supported simplifications, and the next changes. Research unsupported key details first rather than filling them with placeholder blocks and calling them complete.

When a style is already approved, art review must check the [actual complete composite against the design board](visual-system.md#actual-implementation-of-approved-designs). Also check [hiding and small-displacement evidence](visual-system.md#three-scale-form-and-production-detail) for layered assets intended for use. Delivery review additionally checks output purposes under the [G2 delivery scope](production-checkpoints.md#actual-g1g7-progression-records). A local conclusion commissioned only for science or technical checks must not be expanded into art approval.

For Blender video, also check [scene background and spatial finish](engineering-blender.md#blender-backgrounds-and-spatial-completion): the actual picture must support the overall environment, local simplification scope, contact/scale/lighting, and cross-shot changes. Placeholder backgrounds, or a persistently uniform background without narrative purpose, are returned under existing G3/G5 rules. A refined subject or the number of environment assets cannot compensate.

<a id="用户观看版本与内部代理"></a>
## User-Viewing Versions and Internal Proxies

Internal layout rough cuts, still checks, and low-cost proxies may locate specific issues; they cannot replace short audiovisual samples that play normally. Versions the user uses to judge the opening, rhythm, detail, and fluidity must use the target playback frame rate and genuinely continuous subject states. Check frame rate, dimensions, and unique motion frames separately. Repeated frames, added duration, or changing only the encoded frame rate must not disguise a sampled-frame slideshow as continuous production. Use this film's normal delivery frame-rate target; the low frame rate of one technical experiment is not the series' viewing standard.

Fix known opening defocus, overly slow narration, crude models/materials, and obvious stutter before asking the user to judge choices that remain open. Marking a version “rough cut/awaiting review” states purpose and unverified scope only; it does not exempt known repairable defects or let caption, marking, or cover fixes replace subject repairs. State missing continuous-viewing/listening capability honestly. Still-frame counts, full decoding, matching hashes, ASR, and complete tables cannot sign off the corresponding perceptual quality.

## HD Video Output

Formal renders, G3 picture-quality samples, G4 full candidates, and G5 delivery use an HD target. New work in this series defaults to 1920×1080 landscape; explicitly selected portrait uses 1080×1920. Other aspect ratios retain the selected ratio with a short side of at least 1080 pixels. A user-specified higher size, such as 3840×2160, takes precedence. Do not automatically reduce resolution for speed or lower transfer volume, or upgrade to 4K or 60fps by default. Frame rate follows the film's established target.

Record the episode's single target `{width,height}` in existing `design.output`, both positive integers. New templates default to 1920×1080. Change both together when aspect ratio or resolution is explicitly changed, synchronizing the film's render, caption/annotation layout, and encoding settings. Actual formal output dimensions must match the explicit target. For older records without the field, still check a short side of at least 1080; explicitly migrate to the current target on resumption rather than rewriting historical receipts.

Render formal Blender frames natively at target dimensions and check 100% output; retain that canvas through compositing and final encoding. MoneyPrinterTurbo asset preparation, arrangement, and export follow the same target. Merely enlarging low-resolution pixels does not make an asset HD. Improve/remake the source according to actual shot size, crop, and detail, then check subject, textures, fine lines, and captions at final dimensions. Passing dimensions does not establish detail quality.

Low-resolution `motion_preview`, layout rough cuts, and diagnostic images are for internal technical checks only. Mark purpose, actual dimensions, and unverified scope. Retain normal frame rate and the actions being observed; they do not replace a formal picture-quality sample or final film. Samples used for the user's judgment of formal appearance use this film's formal target. Low-resolution proxies remain proxies and do not overwrite HD masters.

The executor uses local ffprobe to check actual video-stream dimensions, pixel aspect ratio, rotation, and frame rate in formal samples/final films against the episode target. Square pixels, correct aspect ratio, and normal continuous playback must all hold. Reuse existing execution receipts and technical records without new approval. Dimension failures in formal `sample/full` output or final delivery cannot be recorded as execution success or G5 approval. Internal `roughcut` is not subject to the formal minimum dimensions. At normal speed and target viewing scale, compare the master with the actual final encoding and repair softness, blocking, lost detail, and flicker. ffprobe, full decoding, and high bitrate cannot sign off picture quality. Use [final-encoding detail retention](visual-system.md#detail-retention-in-the-final-encoding) to locate scaling, compositing, or compression loss. Return user-review materials as verified clickable local file links under the [link-delivery rule](tool-routing.md#review-media-without-repeated-uploads), without uploading or automatic preview.

## Subject Motion and Long Static Holds

The video must advance the observation or mechanism being explained over actual playback time. A real anchor is only continuous moving live-action/observation footage with verified user capture and authorization for this use, an original interval, and rights evidence. Process explanation must develop the main-picture subject's state, form, position, or interactions with the narration. Caption/title changes, decorative particles, whole-image translation/scaling (including Ken Burns), still-image transitions, and frequent hard cuts between stills do not count as effective subject progression. Fine static illustration and narration cannot replace video; static backgrounds need not be forced into motion.

Check the full timeline separately for the G2 complete voiced rough cut, G3 continuous representative sample, and G5 final complete export. First watch/listen continuously at normal speed, then hide captions/titles/decoration or view only the subject ROI to check whether narration-required changes actually occur. Mark every complete semantic segment and the longest interval without effective subject progression. Cuts, hard cuts, and caption changes do not reset that interval. Mechanical cuts every 2 seconds, sped-up clips, or random micromovement do not repair a failure. Previously rejected versions remain failed in the failure ledger; only review of the corresponding actual new export may close them.

The 3.0-second no-progression and 1.6-second reading values in `config/production-policy.json subject_motion` are starting points that trigger review, not hard requirements for motion every second. Process shots must show promised state development. Purposeless stalling, reliance only on decoration/cuts, or renaming an entire process as reading still fails. At G2, process explanation checks how actions cause object response and results; G3 must cover key interactions and the hardest state changes. Turntables, translation, or slow exploded-view mini-samples cannot replace verification of motion finish for that scope. At normal speed, identify where the action happens, which object responds, and how the result persists. If missing, repair through [shared-state and interaction design](animation-scene-design.md#process-actions-must-leave-observable-results), using existing QA for rechecks. Scientifically necessary observation, comparison, and result holds may be longer, with actual normal-speed viewing/listening explaining what viewers observe, why the time is necessary, and how it returns to the explanation. Do not invent movement to meet a duration or compress necessary reading.

`qa.shotbook.subject_motion` (G2), `qa.visual_frames.animation.subject_motion` (G3), and `qa.visual_frames.subject_motion` (G5) respectively record `status`, the current stage's `context_sha256`, the reviewed `media_sha256`, actual `roi_review`, five categories of `excluded_motion_review`, and immutable `screen {file,sha256}`. `spans` continuously covers the entire reviewed file in seconds. Each entry has `start/end/kind/subject/observation/narration_relation/evidence`; kind is `subject_change`, `authentic_motion`, `purposeful_read`, or `no_progress`. The first two require supported subject changes; holds also record `purpose/resume_evidence`. For purposeful_read exceeding the trigger starting point, add `hold_review={status:pass,reviewer,observation,evidence}` in the same record, binding that entry's existing current media/context. When an entire semantic unit truly serves observation/comparison only, use `task_type=observation` with the same actual review. A process unit cannot omit its process through this classification. `semantic_units` likewise covers the file completely and continuously, recording `start/end/step_ids/spoken_point/progression_observed/evidence`; do not omit openings, endings, or narration without motion. G3 covers the selected explanatory steps; G2/G5 cover all steps. Record calculated `longest_no_progress_seconds`; average frame difference, total movement, an attractive local action, or a high score cannot compensate for the worst interval.

Existing `qa.shotbook.viewing` (G2), `qa.visual_frames.animation.viewing` (G3), and `qa.visual_frames.viewing` (G5) follow the original contract for `method=continuous_playback`, `playback_speed=1`, actual watching/listening, and complete `ranges`. Stills/sampled frames cannot sign off continuous viewing. This continuous-viewing record may explicitly add `frame_scope=full_frame` and `visual_review_capable=true`. `roi_only` means local-only review. Capability fields belong only to the actual reviewer issuing that observation; they do not inherit the assistant's or another reviewer's capability. Fields present must agree with the normal-speed full-frame conclusion signed: a local scope and unknown/missing capability cannot be overridden by `pass` or a watched Boolean. Historical records lacking these two optional fields remain checked under the original contract, without automatic values or forced migration. Explicit null/unknown values cannot count as confirmed. Separately listed diagnostics such as pausing, magnification, sampled frames, or removing auxiliary graphics are outside this acceptance observation and do not recursively inherit this check; they locate issues but cannot replace continuous full-frame observation. The program checks contradictions in declarations only. Complete fields and specific observation text do not establish genuine viewing.

First run `python scripts/subject_motion.py <media> --regions <regions.json> --report <new-screen.json>` on the actual export. regions is a JSON array continuously covering the full file; each entry contains `start/end/subject/isolation_note/roi/masks`. roi and mask.rect are full-frame normalized `[x,y,width,height]`; each mask also has kind (captions/titles/decoration). Choose the complete relevant subject/relationship ROI for the topic, checking that it omits no key subject and does not select decoration as the subject, and that masks do not conceal necessary mechanisms. An empty mask list may explicitly indicate that no masking is needed. Screening samples frame differences within the subject region, lists low-change intervals, and reports the longest candidate interval while fixing media/parameters. The checker decodes again to verify the report, which must also enter that gate's `qa.gates.<stage>.evidence`. Errors, a missing report, stale versions/parameters, or timeline gaps remain unverified and block stage passage.

Automatic frame differences screen only; they cannot identify meaningful causality, authentic live action, Ken Burns, or hard cuts between stills. Actually replay every low-change candidate over the same range. When sampling missed real progression, record `result=effective_subject_progression`. If the whole interval is purposeful observation and corresponding spans are all actually reviewed purposeful_read, use `result=purposeful_observation` with `hold_review`. Both require specific observation/evidence and must not contradict the observed timeline. Purposeless stillness, a missing required process, or inability to actually review continuously remains failed/unverified. Fields do not establish the picture.

Return for a missing promised process, purposeless stalling, falsely claimed observation, distorted ROI/masks, unresolved screening candidates, or missing continuous viewing/listening of the current version. Supported structural observation is not wrong solely because of its duration.

Within the subject-ROI review above, also diagnose object results: temporarily hide added labels, ticks, and explanatory overlays, inspect subject states/interactions/results that should be visible under this segment's scientific model, then restore necessary diagrams to check correspondence. Hiding is diagnostic; it does not remove necessary scientific graphics from the final film. Do not mask the subject or necessary mechanism being inspected to manufacture a pass. Invisible quantities such as temperature/water vapor and abstract relationships may be explained through diagrams; invisible quantities need not become visible to the eye, nor must static environments move. However, a segment promising a visible object result cannot be renamed “diagram only” to hide a static sticker. Motion only in overlays with the subject's required result persistently absent remains a failure.

Packages newly created or re-reviewed under this version use `workflow_version=3`, recording `status/media_sha256/units` in existing `subject_motion.object_result_review`. Each entry fixes `start/end/step_ids` in the same order as original `semantic_units` and records `status`, `mode=visible_result/diagram_only`, and `subject/overlay_isolation/observation/scientific_boundary/evidence`, adding `diagram_only_reason` for diagram-only entries. Evidence text must locate current media, subject ROI, and actual times. Actual review judges scientific invisibility, static environments, and the reasonableness of necessary diagrams; changing mode alone cannot pass. Reuse current media, subject ROI, and continuous review for diagnosis without a new gate. Tools check fields, ranges, and current-media consistency only; they do not read the actual picture referenced in evidence text or judge art. Unchanged historical packages at `workflow_version=2` (or without a declared version) and wholly lacking this item need no forced migration. If the item appears, verify it completely; null is not a compatibility mode, and deleting it in version3 still blocks passage. Workflow version enters the new stage digest; do not downgrade and reuse an old pass record.

### Moving animatics and user review

For an animation commission, including 3D video, distinguish an internal narrated storyboard from a moving rough cut. Still boards and explicit placeholders can establish script order internally. A rough cut sent for judgment of rhythm, motion, appearance or the viewing experience must represent the required subject actions and shot handoffs across the commissioned timeline. Simplify geometry, materials, lighting or simulation for a normal-frame-rate blocking pass; do not replace missing actions with prolonged still boards. A few animated opening seconds cannot establish the remainder of the film.

Before that handoff, the parent/editor checks the existing timeline and actual source implementation: which intervals use native moving frames, held frames, image composites or repeated loops, and whether each spoken unit's required change is represented. A full-duration MP4, camera movement, moving road/background, overlay animation or changing captions cannot offset missing explanatory progression. Reuse existing source manifests, event mappings and continuous observations; inspect local repeated-frame/hold information only as needed. This is the existing G2/G3/G5 motion responsibility applied before requesting feedback, not a new approval gate, a per-frame review quota or an automatic stillness score. Source checks establish what was assembled; they cannot approve its audiovisual quality.

A deliberate freeze or static comparison remains valid when it serves a specific observation task, has enough context, and leads to a meaningful next observation/action. Record its purpose and return in the existing timeline, applying the hold-review method above. Do not force unrelated motion into it, turn a static comparison into a fictitious physical process, or animate different outcomes where the scientific point requires identical displays. Declaring a whole static body to be an observation task does not establish that its duration or viewing experience works.

Known missing action, inherited freeze shortcuts or a prior user rejection must return to the responsible motion/edit owner before another candidate is offered for the same viewing judgment. Labeling it rough, pending, decodable or not yet heard does not waive a known picture failure. The parent changes the implementation/shot plan and reviews the affected continuous result; passing a new metadata check cannot close the defect. If the user explicitly requests the storyboard, failed version or diagnostic file itself, provide that specific material with its actual limited scope. Such a request does not approve it as the moving rough cut or authorize a change of medium. Never rewrite old receipts or hot-switch active jobs during a Skill update.

## Reviewers and Viewing Safety

Each review records the actual reviewer/role, available image-viewing/continuous-playback/listening capabilities, reviewed files/intervals, and uncovered parts. Complete required normal-speed continuous coverage for the current responsibility and stage without using production explanations midway to fill gaps. Locate additional diagnosis through [risk-based review](#risk-based-rechecks-and-evidence-reuse). An editor's self-review after reading the script is not a first viewing by an unfamiliar audience. An independent task identity is neither a human test nor proof of actual audiovisual perception. Prepare review materials through [local selection and deduplicated transfers](tool-routing.md#review-media-without-repeated-uploads). Frame-level diagnosis does not require submitting every raw frame of an entire segment to a model; without continuous perception, many still-image calls cannot masquerade as complete viewing.

Match each observation method to its conclusion scope. Key states/sampled frames support only actually seen moments; local/auxiliary-removed views support only the observed object; target-size stills support only that presentation. They cannot be promoted respectively to continuous processes, original-composition full-frame relationships, or normal-speed understanding. In existing continuous-viewing records, first record the objects, changes, and preceding/following causal links actually noticed in the same semantic segment at target display size with normal-speed full-frame playback, then use pausing, magnification, or removal of auxiliary graphics to diagnose differences. Restating a script or expected answer is not actual observation. Without the corresponding perceptual tool, authorized production may still create a playable candidate, leaving missing items unverified and known failures in their original ledger. Technical fixes or new unknowns cannot clear a failure or justify a fabricated pass.

For rapid luminance changes, repeated high-contrast flashes, or full-frame light/dark alternation, first reduce unnecessary strong flashes and express the change gently without scientific distortion. Review the actual assets and complete composited timeline. Frame rate, a few sampled frames, or average brightness cannot establish absence of risk throughout, and warning text does not replace repair. If a suspected risk remains unresolved or the corresponding continuous-check/detection capability is missing, keep the item unverified and do not sign “safe to view.” Report limitations when specialized assessment is needed; do not provide a medical safety guarantee.

The user's completed review of the exact current version, actual interval, and specific items may serve as corresponding evidence, preserving genuine sources and uncovered parts. The assistant's own inability to hear audio must not permanently freeze other authorized production or invalidate genuine user review. Missing continuous viewing/listening still remains honestly pending; do not convert still observations, retellings, or technical results into a claim of having listened.

## Risk-Based Rechecks and Evidence Reuse

Within the same version/responsibility, maintain only one actual semantic timeline and set of observed facts. Existing storyboards, review logs, and file references may be shared sources. Existing validation views such as `spans`, `semantic_units`, and `object_result_review.units` may be derived from that timeline with their distinct conclusions, without requiring reviewers to reenter ranges or rewatch the same segment for every field. Derived IDs/ranges/hashes must match current media. Observations, hidden-overlay diagnostics, and other tasks not actually completed remain unverified; generated records cannot automatically become pass.

Distinguish automated completeness checks, normal-speed continuous audiovisual review, and local frame-by-frame diagnosis. Complete stage-required continuous viewing and whole-film watching/listening for a new full final version as usual. G5 explanation and expression responsibilities retain their own independent actual reviews. For the same reviewer, responsibility, media version, context, and covered scope, one genuine viewing may support multiple existing QA items with separate observations and conclusions. Do not replay the entire film for each form field. Another reviewer's conclusion cannot replace independent viewing required by this responsibility, and reuse cannot erase old failures.

Use pausing, local magnification, key states, and frame-level diagnosis according to actual risk: new objects/mechanisms, contact and occlusion, sections/orientation, fast action/flashes, caption/annotation extremes, encoding questions, automated-screening candidates, known failures, and effects of this change. Repair rechecks cover the complete change cycle and adjacent joins; expand the review if issues outside that scope are found. Beginning/middle/end sampled frames and thumbnail strips are optional technical aids. There is no per-shot or per-frame manual-review, model-call, or upload quota. Numeric trajectories and file integrity may automatically cover all required frames; this does not equal frame-by-frame visual review. Missing continuous perception remains unverified and cannot be filled by more still-image calls.

Retain conclusions and reuse grounds in existing QA and failure records, without new review packages, approvals, or mandatory checklists. Changes in content, context, or responsibility scope invalidate only affected evidence. A new complete film still requires full watching/listening. Run technical checks at the deepest applicable entry needed by the current task: `delivery` for local delivery, `publish_ready` only with a publication commission. Do not repeatedly run equivalent final checks just to repeat checkmarks. Earlier actually needed G2/G3 checks and expansion limits remain. Files the executor still requires to be checked live must follow that contract; caches or old receipts cannot bypass it. Distinct checks of assets, raw frames, and final encoding cannot compensate for one another. Local processing and outbound materials continue to follow necessity, minimum sufficient material, deduplication, and the hard bandwidth constraint.

## Handling Failures

Return the relevant production stage when the above defects appear, revoke the version's related old passes, and retain issues and before/after repair evidence. If whole-film animation expression has failed, return to explanatory storyboarding and a motion plan covering the complete explanatory process. Local motion approval or repair retains only that local approval scope; it cannot clear the overall failure. Verify the plan first with a continuous representative segment, then recheck the whole film rather than replacing one action and carrying over an old pass. When the user explicitly rejects a batch as a whole, freeze the batch's current “deliverable/publishable” status. Change affected `qa` items to `pending` (awaiting recheck) or `fail` (confirmed failure). Find common root causes, review every quality dimension, then recheck a small number of representative samples. Repairing only named local issues cannot restore the batch's overall pass.

After dissatisfaction, work through the original QA defect record:

1. **Locate**: bind original feedback, version, and interval; distinguish content, joins, picture, sound, or layout. If the user did not limit the issue locally, inspect the affected whole film for the same mechanism. List changes, retained items, and unverified items.
2. **Repair**: change the responsible normative text only if a rule is missing or ambiguous; when rules are already clear, fix actual execution. Follow the [current commission](production-checkpoints.md#3-stage-progression-and-representative-samples) for scope and retain matching valid tracks/assets.
3. **Recheck**: view the repaired segment, dependent shots, and joins. A complete new version still requires full watching/listening. Preserve the original failure and pre-repair evidence; only actual review of the current repaired version may close it.
4. **Retain the lesson**: synchronize general improvements to rules and necessary regressions; keep private feedback/media in the project. Mark future works as awaiting validation until produced and watched/listened to. Skill changes do not replace media repair.

This process does not automatically revive, modify, or republish published, canceled, or prohibited content.

## Full-Quality Diagnostic Scores and Internal Explanation Review

When comparing approaches or reviewing outcomes, the five-dimensional diagnostic score in [production policy](../config/production-policy.json) may be used. Each dimension is 0–5, with half points allowed: 0 confirmed absent, 1 major error, 2 obvious obstruction, 3 usable but rough, 4 complete and mature, 5 a benchmark. Record specific times/evidence for the current version. Unfilled scores or empty templates do not block a stage; quality items not actually reviewed remain unverified.

| Dimension / weight | Score-4 anchor | Score-5 anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understanding and narrative 30 | Core question, necessary explanatory relationships, and answer form a clear closure that normal viewing can follow | A small amount of explanation accurately shows the key mechanism, with clear value on rewatching |
| Art consistency 25 | Whole/medium/close views have consistent form, materials, and layering, without obvious collage | Mature composition and series recognition suitable as a visual baseline |
| Action and camera 25 | Core changes are readable, objects remain continuous, and timing/pauses are appropriate | Precise, natural actions with designed detail that adds no comprehension burden |
| Sound 15 | Natural and clear, without obvious joins, with picture/sound correspondence | Narration, environment, and action form a stable, restrained rhythm |
| Captions and packaging 5 | Readable on a phone, no mechanism obstruction, accurate cover | Captions/covers/series language are unified and improve understanding |

Calculate the weighted total as the sum of score÷5×weight; it only helps locate gaps. The duplicate routine-85/benchmark-90 and minimum-dimension thresholds are canceled. Do not manufacture records to reach scores. Voluntarily entered values, arithmetic, evidence, and media versions must still be genuine and consistent. Final release depends on existing science/rights, L3 finish, visible relationships, complete actual audiovisual review, closure of known failures, and this task's applicable scope. High scores cannot replace them, and an absent scorecard cannot overturn completed actual review.

At G5, qa.explanation_review and qa.expression_review record actual evidence from the current complete final export for each responsibility. Detailed existing qa.comprehension.logic_review / internal_review records may map to the same stage responsibilities; do not require a duplicate set of approvals. They may cite the same actual viewing and stepwise record, with each responsibility's conclusion, scope, and version still explicit.

G2 accepts the corresponding scope through plan review and actual full rough-cut evidence. G5 fixes actual_information_only=true, independent_of_generation_context=true, and media_sha256, recording reviewer_kind, visual_review_capable, audio_review_capable, and complete viewing. Preserve conclusions/evidence for understanding_goals answer, relationships, transfer, and boundaries separately. Reuse evidence under [risk-based rechecks](#risk-based-rechecks-and-evidence-reuse). Record actual review scope, device, whether sound was included, and perceptual limits. When this task includes covers, G5 compares the current covers, opening, most abstract segment, and ending side by side at target size, then watches the relevant segments and whole film at normal speed. Compare subject distinguishability, scene finish, and whole/detail reconnection. Different aspect ratios need not share the same composition. `video_only` uses an applicable style reference without fabricating a cover for acceptance.

Scene failures belong to A03/A08, D01/D07, and I01/I04: small-screen key states are indistinguishable, details lose whole-object orientation, or narration describes changes while main picture lacks the process/comparison. Repair shot size, relationships, and scene organization before surface treatment, then rewatch affected continuous segments.

Locate other failures in specific items: A01/A02 proportions/contact; A03/A08 competing backgrounds/edges; A04 wrong color semantics; D02/I04 unconnected joins; A06/A07/A10/D05 encoding detail loss, merging, or flicker. Repair the responsible step rather than changing levels or overlaying filters.

Grayscale, shrinking, blur, and local magnification are diagnostic views. Grayscale alone cannot judge distinguishability of equal-value colors, and thumbnail strips cannot judge full-film duration or action quality. Whole-film rhythm still requires continuous playback with the real track. If fixed sampling misses key states, transitions, or motion extremes, view the corresponding actual segment. Three similar frames do not establish no motion, and three harmonious frames cannot approve L3.

Free-text `note` / `evidence` locates observations; the checker does not parse file links inside it. Add each page record, comparison image/video, HTML thumbnail page, manifest, and extracted frame that needs binding individually to the corresponding `qa.gates.<stage>.evidence` `{file, sha256}` list. Recording only a Markdown, HTML, or manifest hash does not recursively bind referenced content. Paths are rooted at the episode directory. Evidence must actually exist and match current bytes. A generated manifest alone does not establish review.

After stage approval, retain the evidence snapshot from that time. Later stages write new versions, such as separate G2/G3/G5 art records and extracted-frame directories, rather than modifying one report and invalidating prior gate digests. After changes to media, shot times, key states, or design references, rebuild affected static evidence and review the actual affected scope. Even QA-text-only changes alter the original episode hash used at generation; distinguish that from storyboard/media changes. Existing in-scope design approval may be reused rather than requesting the same approval for new records.

## Autonomous Explanation and Expression Review

Assign reviewers with actual capabilities according to the stage records above. Both G5 reviews are independent of generation context: **explanation review** checks the question, premises, reasoning, and applicable conditions; **expression review** checks whether those relationships actually appear in the work. Judge only information presented up to that point, record the first breakdown, and do not fill gaps with the author's explanations.

Check whether the work supports answering the original question, explaining key relationships, judging small changes that require no new knowledge, and recognizing applicable conditions for conclusions/diagrams. Paper scripts support logic review; the final work requires complete actual watching/listening. Real-audience testing is optional.

| Clause / check | Actual check | Repair on failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 Goal and E06 closure | Under [whole-film direction](viewer-retention-design.md#direct-the-whole-film-before-polishing-its-parts), check the overall context, episode question, role of examples, segment-by-segment progression, and ending actually conveyed. Promises require scientific and visible grounds. First restate from the work “what the film asks and how it reaches the answer,” then compare with the commission; titles/author explanations cannot fill gaps | Missing overall orientation, an example replacing the topic, or a promise mismatching evidence/conclusions returns to the complete script and affected edit. Added background sentences, explanatory text, or polished voice tone alone cannot repair it |
| Opening context and reason to watch | Does the first screen and complete opening make the object, use/context, and real point of interest recognizable, give a reason to continue, and naturally enter the original question and body? A title, question mark, or first-sentence answer cannot replace actual motivation | If it starts with terms/processes without context, add the whole and the concrete difficulty. If setup drags, remove purposeless background. Retain useful information rather than substituting empty “震撼/你知道吗” or rigid seconds |
| Opening-to-body entry | Continuously watch/listen at normal speed until the first body observation/explanation is established. Can viewers say why they are looking here now, how objects/scales correspond after the switch, and which specific opening question it answers? Does the first body sentence advance rather than restart? | Record the first breakdown and adjacent shots/times. Add the missing reason, orientation, or mapping. If moving back from a finished product to manufacturing, establish the time change. Unsupported deformation, dissolves, or continuous music cannot compensate for broken understanding |
| E02 Premises | Are objects, concepts, and references established before they are needed? | Add necessary premises and orientation |
| E03–E05 Structure and reasoning | Are there major jumps from premise to conclusion? Do segments connect according to comprehension tasks and continuously supply evidence/relationships rather than delay the answer? | Add intermediate relationships and reorder segments. Remove purposeless previews/repetition/branches rather than merely adding “所以” or speeding up |
| E07 Expression fit | Does the method show the actual difficulty? | Change the demonstration rather than only polishing narration |
| E08 Key picture | Does the core picture carry observation of orientation, comparison, tracking, change, structure, or constraints? | Add states, connections, or comparisons required by the relationship. Related objects/keywords/effects are not explanation |
| E09 Following path | After changes in object, viewpoint, space/time, or scale, is the viewed content still understood? | Add anchors and correspondence. Teaching scans/decomposition/pauses must not pass as actual event order |
| E10 Channel coordination | Narration guides observation/connects reasoning; main picture supplies observation grounds; labels locate/disambiguate; captions accurately preserve narration | Match key meaning with objects/changes in time. Do not require animation for each word or force viewers to assemble relationships across space/time |
| E11 Simplification boundaries | Do simplification/analogy/simulation retain core relationships, object mappings, and necessary explanations of differences? | Prevent schematic lines being mistaken for physical objects, teaching animation for factual evidence, correlation for causation, or conditional conclusions for universal ones |
| E12 Misleading implications | What core misunderstanding is most likely even after attentive viewing? | Resolve ambiguity through comparisons/qualifications/return to real context rather than defending author intent |

Picture and sound jointly establish necessary correspondence; the silent picture need not independently explain every detail. Return to design when an answer depends on unpresented steps, picture and sound conflict, or extra explanation from the author is required.

Use the existing defect ledger for rework records: specific location/version → missing condition for understanding → expression to change → actual evidence for recheck. Unclear objects require recognition/premise checks; unclear relationships require layout/connection/comparison checks; locally understood but unconnected parts require reasoning/continuity checks; inability to keep up requires segmentation/holds/appearance-order checks; viewpoint confusion requires anchors; false conclusions require analogy boundaries; inability to answer the original question requires hierarchy/integration. “More accessible” or “more intuitive” is not a complete rework instruction.

At G1, check the opening's overall context, real point of interest, viewing benefit, scientific grounds, and body connection in existing note/evidence within `qa.explanation_logic`. At G2, `qa.expression_plan` continuously watches/listens from the first frame through the opening until the first body observation/explanation is established, recording absent motivation before explanation, ineffective delay, or restarting after entry. Then check whether the first useful information, relationship progression, observation holds, and answer actually hold. G3 uses existing representative-segment evidence to recheck appeal and fulfillment for covered relationships, without promoting local results to whole-film conclusions. At G5, `qa.explanation_review` / `qa.expression_review` recheck the current complete final export from opening to ending and every promise. Record specific locations, what was seen/heard then, the first loss of tracking or lack of progression, changes, and actual-review results. Independent review follows existing requirements for different task contexts, without new appeal scores, fixed seconds, or parallel approvals. After publication, record platform results through [actual retrospective review](production-checkpoints.md#capacity-cost-and-retrospectives). Every result must point to a location in the reviewed work. Repair known failures and check again. Missing optional human feedback does not block passage; missing actually required audiovisual checks still blocks the corresponding final-film approval.

Visible-text purpose classification, the ban on production excuses, and protection of necessary notices follow the [sole normative text responsibility](visual-system.md#visible-text-purposes-and-production-excuses). Whole-episode tone and cross-segment continuous listening follow the [sole normative voice responsibility](voice-reference.md#whole-episode-narration-continuity).
