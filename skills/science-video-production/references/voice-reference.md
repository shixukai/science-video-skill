## Check the script before full-episode synthesis

Full narration synthesis uses the current complete script integrated by the parent, with the existing G1 paper review completed under [whole-film direction](viewer-retention-design.md#direct-the-whole-film-before-polishing-its-parts). The voice author first reads the relevant brief and complete script, checking whether viewers can identify this episode's question, why the local case appears, and whether the opening promise matches the conclusion. Return obvious disconnections to the script with concrete revisions before synthesizing a long track for the user to discover the problem. An authorized author may draft revisions; the parent owns whole-film integration. The voice role does not become the default director.

Approval of a timbre/speaking-rate sample supports only the corresponding voice direction, not the complete script or film. Independent listening samples and technical feasibility tests need not wait for the full script. Existing scripts/evidence may be referenced, and narrative approval adds no user approval stage. A paper review cannot masquerade as listening; actual voice and full audiovisual media still receive actual review at subsequent stages. After script revisions, update only affected audio, captions and action timing, without recreating unrelated assets.

### Edit Mandarin for the ear

Edit the complete spoken draft against the intended shot order during the existing G1 read.

1. **Find lines that do no work.** Use a local phrase search or a Chinese editing aid to mark possible title echoes, empty questions and directions (“想知道……？先看……”, “接下来我们来看看”), repeated sentence patterns and recap slogans. Treat each hit as a suggestion. Give the opening a specific event, observation or unresolved problem. Let the cut locate the next object; use the handoff to add an observation, cause or constraint. Keep questions, sequence words and comparisons that actually help the explanation.
2. **Revise for speech and check meaning.** Read from the opening through the first body observation, then read the changed passages at the intended pace. Use concrete subjects and verbs, natural pauses and the accepted series voice. If a line could move unchanged to an unrelated episode and does no work here, cut or replace it. Preserve technical terms that matter, source attribution, order, conditions and certainty; never turn a planned demonstration into a verified result. Recheck the affected interval in the existing audiovisual review. A text scan or read-through is not actual listening, and no detector score, separate report or user audition is required.

Editorial references: [Humanizer-zh](https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh) for preserving meaning and register, and [lieflat-less-ai-tone](https://github.com/larashero3-dotcom/lieflat-less-ai-tone) for separating mechanical hints from contextual judgment. These are reading references, not required tools or bundled code.

## Continuous narration and synthesis units

The following method applies to the confirmed project, service and optional adapter. Within an episode, default to generating the master track from the full continuous narration script, then arrange shots by its actual meaning and pauses. A cut, caption line break or storyboard row is not an independent synthesis unit. The opening, causal progression and ending belong to one complete narration. Global style instructions must not restart a first-sentence opening delivery on every call; emphasis follows the current meaning.

Check the instructions actually in effect before generation. Remove or revise requirements that restart the introduction or finish prematurely on every call, recording actual changes. Before synthesis, sanitize script punctuation and clause structure for stable prosody: avoid stacked exclamation/question marks (`！？`/`！！`), theatrical ellipses, unexpanded Latin/unit symbols or overloaded nested sentences that trigger sudden pitch spikes or announcer-style tone shifts in `subtalker` sampling; use natural commas and periods to pace breathing and emphasis. Do not regenerate an accepted track that still matches the script merely because instruction text changed.

On initial generation, split only when the full script encounters a verified interface-length limit or actual generation-stability problem. Split at complete semantic sections, recording the reason, section order and each generation record in the episode. Boundaries fall at complete meanings and natural pauses, never inside a causal connection, reference or sentence. Sections in the same episode retain confirmed model-version evidence, voice, language, style instructions, runtime configuration and obtainable effective generation parameters. Truthfully state unexposed parameters; do not invent a style for each shot or improvise parameter changes. Record the seed strategy actually adopted; identical seeds or parameters cannot guarantee perceptual consistency. First generate and continuously listen to adjacent semantic sections, then expand after confirming that their connection works. One isolated sample cannot establish whole-episode continuity.

Locate local word or delivery problems in the actual track first. Because `CustomVoice` has no cross-call acoustic wave memory, every separate synthesis call initializes a fresh prosodic state. Avoid patchwork micro-splicing (pasting 3+ cropped short retakes into an existing track): when multiple local sections change or a cropped retake exhibits a register/timbre seam against neighboring speech, prefer regenerating the complete continuous script (or complete semantic chapter) in one call with the confirmed parameters and seed. When a scoped retake is used, regenerate a complete semantic section containing the problem and necessary preceding/following context, preferably replacing the whole section. If cutting is actually necessary, choose natural low-energy pauses and continuously listen across both joins; do not paste isolated words/sentences back directly. Verify what text or supported voice conditioning actually reached the model. `context_before/context_after` notes alone are not synthesis context, and an interface must not be credited with cross-request voice memory it does not provide. If extra context is generated and then cropped, record the real inputs and edits through a supported source chain; do not label cropped output as an untouched full segment. Reuse already accepted portions that still match, preserve the new version and actual processing under [run records and audio binding](#run-records-and-audio-binding), then realign affected picture, audio and captions.

## One narration master and one shared processing chain

The series profile's `audio.mix_preset` owns the whole-narration processing default `mandarin-continuous-master-v1`. For new or revised narration, build one continuous source/master before arranging picture cuts. All visual shots reference that same track and shared narration bus; a cut must not reset gain, pitch, speed, EQ, compression, denoising or voice conditioning. Keep source narration, processed voice stem and final mix distinct. Preserve the model-native source and matching accepted tracks; a new preset does not authorize automatic reprocessing, synthesis or repeated user auditions.

| Scope | Default and actual use |
| --- | --- |
| Narration master | Target `-18 LUFS`, true-peak ceiling `-2 dBTP`; one measured constant gain for the complete dry narration, followed by a single 48 kHz FLOAT WAV output with channels preserved |
| Voice character | No pitch shift, speed change or per-shot normalization. Natural Mandarin tones, register variation and meaningful emphasis remain; equal pitch is not the target |
| Optional processing | EQ, compressor, denoiser and limiter bypass by default for clean TTS. If actual defects need another agreed chain, keep it shared across the episode, record it and listen; do not improvise a processor on each shot |
| Final mix | `production-policy.json defaults.mix`: target `-16 LUFS` with its `1 LU` calibration tolerance and true-peak ceiling `-1.5 dBTP`, measured on the complete new/revised final export after music/effects. This is a different scope from the narration stem |

Use an applicable accepted project-specific recipe when it takes precedence; record its actual values instead of silently alternating between presets. To prepare a new master locally from a complete source, use [prepare_voice_master.py](../scripts/prepare_voice_master.py) with existing FFmpeg/ffprobe binaries:

```sh
python -X utf8 scripts/prepare_voice_master.py \
  --episode-dir <absolute-episode-directory> \
  --input <complete-dry-narration.wav> \
  --output <new-narration-master.wav> \
  --record <new-master-postprocess.json> \
  [--boundaries <sec1,sec2,...>] \
  --ffmpeg <existing-ffmpeg-binary> --ffprobe <existing-ffprobe-binary>

# Read-only preflight on any source, retake or assembled WAV before mastering/editing:
python -X utf8 scripts/prepare_voice_master.py \
  --analyze-only --episode-dir <absolute-episode-directory> \
  --input <candidate-or-assembled.wav> [--boundaries <sec1,sec2,...>]
```

The helper measures source integrated loudness/true peak, applies one constant gain limited by peak headroom, measures the actual output, and records `continuity_diagnostics` (windowed active-speech RMS spread, max adjacent level jump, fundamental-frequency `f0_median_hz` and semitone jumps across windows/boundaries, and isolated high-crest transient peaks). It does not dynamically compress to force the loudness target, alter pitch/speed, synthesize speech or normalize individual shots. Peak-limited target shortfall and any `continuity_diagnostics.flags` (`adjacent_window_level_jump`, `wide_episode_level_spread`, `pitch_register_jump`, `boundary_level_or_pitch_jump`, `isolated_transient_limiting_gain`) remain explicit review items rather than a retry loop or hidden processor. Native-source identity, actual preset/commands/gain, output format, continuity diagnostics and before/after measurements belong in its single processing record. The [FFmpeg loudnorm documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#loudnorm) distinguishes measurement and normalization; requesting linear normalization alone can still fall back to dynamic mode, so the helper uses its measurement values and a separate constant-gain output.

For necessary segmented synthesis, first retain the verified complete native PCM concatenation and its `concatenate_full_segments` record. Pass that record with `--upstream-record <native-concatenation-record.json>`; the resulting `whole_track_linear_gain` record binds its source to that assembly, automatically inspects segment-boundary level and pitch jumps when native segment WAVs are present, and keeps `upstream_record={file,sha256}`. The manifest still binds original generation/configuration, input text and ordered native segments. This optional one-step mastering path preserves duration, channels and seam times while allowing the declared sample-rate/PCM-format conversion. Other cuts, overlaps, speed/pitch changes or arbitrary processing do not gain permission from this path. The current final master and its actual listening evidence remain the episode's bound narration.

Whole-track LUFS and true peak do not prove stable timbre/register or every local level. Before picture assembly, run or inspect `continuity_diagnostics` across retake/synthesis seams and shot handoffs, then verify against the comparison anchor by actual listening:
- **Timbre, pitch register or speaking-style shift (`pitch_register_jump` / boundary `semitone_jump > 2.0` or audible voice change)**: gain or EQ cannot repair prosody or register drift. Retake the complete contextual chapter or regenerate the full continuous script in one pass; do not disguise it with pitch flattening, speed changes or a limiter.
- **Local level step while voice character and register stay consistent (`adjacent_window_level_jump` / boundary `level_jump_db > 2.5 dB`)**: apply a smooth linear-dB gain envelope with transition knots placed strictly inside measured low-energy silence valleys on the clean source before running `prepare_voice_master.py`, recording the exact knot times/gains in the source chain.
- **Isolated transient spike starving whole-track loudness (`isolated_transient_limiting_gain` leaving integrated loudness `> 2 LU` below target)**: tame only the pinpointed millisecond consonant spike in a recorded pre-master source step (or apply one shared gentle whole-track compressor in the final mix candidate), never per-shot normalization.
Rebuild the affected master from native sources rather than stacking repeated normalization, and never slice or re-normalize audio per visual shot during video editing or local visual repair. Reuse covered observations under risk-based review; do not add a per-visual-cut audio ledger. The helper's technical result always leaves listening unreviewed, and current source/final-mix listening remains required.

## Listen separately to source audio and the final mix

In `narration.source_listen_review`, record the actual track's file/hash, scope, heard ranges, method/capability, equipment and evidence. A short-sample approval covers only its version and scope; listen again after material voice changes. Private samples and feedback stay with the project.

After segmentation or retakes, continuously listen to the complete meanings and natural pauses of adjacent sections, checking unjustified changes in timbre, rate, emphasis and sentence endings/onsets. Voice-only listening may locate audio problems first; then judge handoffs with picture. Preserve normal emphasis and emotional variation. Record seam timecodes, heard defects and repair results. Failures return to their synthesis/joining step, followed by listening again to affected continuous intervals and the complete mix.

Check each section against the script for omissions/repetition/wrong words, terminology, numbers, units, polyphonic characters and phrasing. Investigate abnormal durations or suspected deviations before assembly. Rewrite long sentences first and arrange observation pauses around the picture. Every video retains Mandarin narration; captions and music do not replace it.

For the final mix, listen to the entire current export at normal speed in `qa.audio.listening`. Complete headphone and phone-speaker checks under the [production policy](../config/production-policy.json), recording equipment, ranges and problem timecodes; each missing check remains unreviewed. Also check key sections on the platform. Mispronounced core terms, swallowed/missing causal words, phrasing that changes meaning, abrupt joins, persistent noise/distortion and music masking explanation require rework. Keep narration, music, ambience and sound effects on separate tracks with speech priority; check transients, clipping and abrupt cutoffs within/across sections. Do not hide problems with whooshes or alarms, add effects by default at every cut, or present designed sound as original recorded audio.

After edits, retakes, word changes, pauses or rate changes, realign timecodes, captions and key actions, then listen to the current full mix again. Reference existing projects or generation records without reinstalling models or changing runtimes merely to populate fields.
