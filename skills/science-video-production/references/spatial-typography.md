# Letter markers only in 3D picture

## Scope and prohibition

This rule applies to 3D videos and the 3D shots of an explicitly mixed video. Ordinary synchronized narration and sound-accessibility subtitles remain a clear editable 2D reading layer; realistic/2D videos and separately commissioned covers keep their existing rules.

**No explanatory text in the 3D picture.** Only a necessary single-letter object/variable marker, such as A, B or α, may appear. Chinese text, English words/abbreviations, phrases, sentences, numbers, units, equations, legends, scene/process headings, scale/model notes and explanation plaques are prohibited. Making them short, important, scientifically correct, physically engraved or rendered in 3D does not permit them. This replaces the previous exception for necessary auxiliary labels/values/conditions.

Each permitted marker identifies one actual referent. Do not split a word or sentence into separate letter items/objects, arrange letters into an explanatory word, or put a text legend beside the letters. Do not move forbidden explanations to another corner, banner, plaque, in-scene display or recurring title card. A letter is optional, not a quota: use no marker when framing and action already identify the target.

Show the relationship with action, before/change/after states, close-ups, scale transitions, meaningful comparison and motivated shot relationships. Explain names, quantities and necessary scientific conditions through the narration and its genuine synchronized subtitles. Preserve scientific accuracy; lettering is not a substitute for the promised visible process. Existing rights/disclosure obligations use their appropriate established placement; incompatible asset requirements must be resolved without hiding an obligation or inventing on-frame permission.

## Apply current corrections before showing previews

When the user explicitly removes or prohibits picture annotations, apply that correction to the current plan, reused brief and output generator before the next preview. Preserve the old directions as history, with the current user-message/evidence reference and affected scope in the existing migration/work log. A request to continue, keep a model or retain a story does not renew a withdrawn text/layout direction. A later explicit user request for specific text is assessed as a new instruction; the Skill never overrides the user by inventing priority.

The restriction also applies to 3D picture candidates shown in composition boards, comparison boards and presentation canvases. Do not add production/disclaimer headings, case descriptions, explanation strips or unverified subtitle placeholders outside the raw render and call the result compliant because the render itself is clean. Keep process/version/diagnostic notes in the separate work log or link description. A static board has no established synchronized soundtrack; proposed speech belongs in the script, with ordinary subtitles added when the actual audio/timeline exists. Independently commissioned cover/title artwork keeps its own scope.

If review has already identified forbidden text, the parent returns the generator/plan for removal and rechecks the changed output before offering it as the next visual candidate. Do not leave the finding as an unresolved role conflict while continuing to display the same violation. Reuse unaffected renders and audio; this correction does not require a fresh 3D render or full-film review for a clean static layout. Do not erase necessary scientific meaning: retain it in the actual narration/synchronized subtitles or appropriate companion material, with unfinished work still pending.

## Build permitted letters in space

Use actual editable letter geometry or a genuine single-letter marking on the relevant object. Bind it to the referent and observation interval, with coherent perspective, depth, occlusion and lighting. A fixed 2D card, fake bevel or perspective filter is insufficient. A camera-facing letter is allowed when its real scene position and depth remain coherent.

- Use an available legally usable font, a restrained face/bevel/depth and a deliberate matte/satin or appropriate surface material. Set its world dimensions and angle from the actual camera and final phone-size reading; do not copy pixel sizes into scene units.
- Keep the letter near its referent without hiding the action or implying false attachment. Maintain identity, lighting and depth order through movement, hiding/rebinding it when the target leaves or becomes occluded. Lines/arrows retain their existing target and construction rules; they do not justify adding words or numeric labels.
- Keep one continuous stable readable interval through the relevant observation. Motion blur, defocus, glare and entrance/exit animation do not count as reading time. Remove an unreadable marker or repair the shot; never replace it with an explanatory sentence.

Keep the glyph/font/material dependencies editable. A matching 3D letter view layer/pass may reuse unchanged beauty frames when camera, color, depth/holdouts, occlusion and lighting agree. Rerender changed letters and affected shadow/reflection/occlusion dependencies. Ordinary 2D subtitle edits may recompose existing picture. See [Blender execution](blender-execution.md#hand-off-raw-frames-to-the-edit).

## Reuse the existing inventory and actual review

Keep `presentation_text.items` and existing overlay evidence. Every frame-text item in a declared 3D scope, including physical lettering, markings and scientific notices, must satisfy the single-letter restriction. Bind actual `shot_ids`; auxiliary markers use `kind=auxiliary_text`, `representation=spatial_3d`. Genuine object lettering still needs its actual `source_asset_id` and asset/file/hash. Neither category nor prior consent waives the current picture rule.

Mirror actual text, `shot_ids`, `kind` and representation in `observed[]`. Use the existing `spatial_review={status, observation}` for actual letter perspective/depth, referent, material/light and continuous legibility. Inspect the whole current picture and text-producing sources, including text converted to meshes, textures and composited plates. Unknown/untracked text is a failure; hiding it from the inventory is not deletion. Reuse current observations and do not add an approval stage or frame-upload quota.

Ordinary `surface=caption` items in the 3D scope retain `caption_role=spoken_sync` or `sound_accessibility`, the current formal caption file/hash and actual `caption_correspondence` review. Condensed synchronized subtitles and written/spoken unit differences are allowed. A detached label/plaque cannot become a subtitle by moving to an SRT, being near the subtitle region or setting a role flag. Keep one synchronized subtitle layer; remove a second explanatory strip rather than merge it into the subtitles.

The script checks declared 3D shots (`modality=controlled_3d` or `shotbook.realism.medium=three_d`) and item shot bindings. A single Latin/Greek letter is accepted as marker content; words, numbers, punctuation and a single Chinese character are not. Missing/invalid shot bindings cannot opt out of a declared 3D scope. The planned restriction is checked before roughcut/sample/full execution and again against actual overlay evidence; it does not automatically recognize undeclared baked text or prove that individually declared letters were not assembled into a word. Actual picture/source review remains required.

## Reject and repair

During existing G2/G3/G5 review, **any prohibited explanatory text is a mandatory return for removal**. This includes necessary-looking words/values, 3D plaques, physical-label reclassification and a second explanation strip overlapping subtitles. Letter misuse, fake 2D auxiliary text, incorrect binding/depth/material or unreadable intervals also fail. Return item/time and the concrete defect together; remove the text at its real source, reuse unaffected picture/audio and recheck the affected sequence. G3 does not expand and G5 does not deliver with this failure. Preserve earlier failures; scores, old passes and technical success cannot offset it. Missing actual perception remains unreviewed.

These support letter implementation and do not certify the result.
